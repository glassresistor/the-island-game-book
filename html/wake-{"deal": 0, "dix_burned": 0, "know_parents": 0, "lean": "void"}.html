This time you open your eyes to a familiar face you've never seen before: only a boy, early twenties perhaps, physiologically. His face is a little lopsided, the cheekbone flatter on the left than the right. His ears are too big. He looks almost natural.

You haven't spoken for millennia. Your voice comes out a whisper: "Who are you?" Not what you're supposed to ask. Not the first question anyone on Eriophora asks, after coming back.

"I'm yours," he says, and just like that you're a mother.

He doesn't give it time to sink in. "You weren't scheduled, but Chimp wants extra hands on deck. Next build's got a situation." So the chimp is still in control. The chimp is always in control. The mission goes on.

You see the damage in his face — those tiny flipped base-pairs resonating up from the microscopic and bending him just a little off-kilter. He looks like he grew up on a planet. Your reinforcements were supposed to be pristine, built from perfect genes buried deep within Eri's iron-basalt mantle. How far out must you be by now, if even your own building blocks have decayed so?

"Situation?" you ask.

"Contact scenario, maybe," he says. "Sun up ahead. Half lightyear. Chimp thinks — maybe it's talking to us." He shrugs. "No rush. Lotsa time."
